THE OASIS SWATCH

Ways to draw an element that has no emoji. Each is shown at the three sizes the game renders .s at, then dropped into a strip beside its real neighbours — the tiles a player actually sees it next to. Costs are measured builds, not estimates. Option 01 is dead: Island took U+1F3DD, so only 02-04 are live. Current bundle is 12629 B of 13312 — 683 B of headroom, the tree at 151 elements.

WHAT IT LANDS NEXT TO

The problem in one row. Desert, Palm and Island are emoji; Sand is an abstract swatch. An Oasis made of emoji would have to survive being fourth in a line of sand-toned pictograms — and Island has now taken the last one that was going spare.

01

THE DESERT-ISLAND EMOJI

TAKEN BY ISLAND no longer available

Ruled out — Island (Sand + Palm) now ships this emoji. It was the one-line answer and the cheapest thing on this page, at +20 B measured. It was also the only option that was arguably wrong: U+1F3DD is a DESERT ISLAND, land ringed by sea, where an oasis is the inverse figure — water ringed by land. Island is the right owner of it. Kept here so the reasoning survives, and so nobody proposes it a second time.

  • UNAVAILABLE: Island holds U+1F3DD. Two elements cannot share a glyph — the board would show the same tile picture twice.
  • Was the cheapest at +20 B, and legible at 32px.
  • Semantically inverted: sea around land, not sand around water.
  • Its palm silhouette repeats Palm at 32px — and now Island too, both of which are the Oasis's own relatives.
  • No glow. The c/bg branch precedes el.e, so an emoji element cannot carry --g — it would sit dark beside Sand and Field.
02

THE HORIZON BAND

+27 B measured

The field idiom, reused exactly: one linear-gradient, a hard stop, sand above and water below. Field already teaches the player that a two-band swatch means "a place, seen side on", so this costs nothing to learn.

  • Precedent is in the table already — Field is this exact shape.
  • Glows, and the glow is water-blue: it reads as wet at a glance.
  • Scales perfectly. A hard-stop gradient is size-independent.
  • Cheapest of the three live options.
  • Reads as a SHORE — water meeting land at a line — which is a beach, not an oasis. The enclosure is the whole idea and it is missing.
  • Nearly indistinguishable from Field in shape at 32px; only the colour separates them.
03

THE POOL IN THE SAND

+43 B measured

A radial-gradient ellipse of water sitting on Sand's own diagonal ground — literally Sand's swatch with a pool cut into it. This is the only option whose figure is the definition of the word: water enclosed by sand.

  • Semantically exact — enclosure, not adjacency.
  • Visibly a descendant of Sand, which is half its ancestry.
  • Glows water-blue and scales cleanly to 110px.
  • Distinct from every emoji neighbour at 32px, which is more than option 02 can claim against Field.
  • 16 B more than the horizon for a difference some players will never consciously register.
  • The ellipse must stay clear of the 6px corner radius at 32px or it clips — the geometry here is already inside that margin.
04

THE DRAWN OASIS

+84 B measured

Inline SVG on the 0 0 32 32 viewBox, the matter route: a pool, a trunk, two fronds. The only option that says "oasis" with no interpretation required — and it costs 12% of the remaining headroom.

  • Unmistakable at 62px and 110px. Nothing else reads as clearly.
  • Vector, so the discovery hero is genuinely sharp.
  • +84 B — four times what the emoji would have cost, against 683 B left in the budget.
  • At 32px the fronds collapse into a dark smudge above a blue bar. Look at the leftmost cell: that is the size it ships at most.
  • Reintroduces the palm silhouette that option 01 was faulted for, only now hand-drawn — and Island is right beside it.

WHERE THIS COMES OUT

Option 03, the pool — and the case got stronger, because the cheap escape hatch is gone. Island took U+1F3DD, so there is no emoji to retreat to if the budget tightens: the Oasis is a swatch or it is nothing. 03 is the only option whose shape means what the word means, it inherits Sand's ground so its ancestry is visible, it glows where an emoji could not, and at +43 B it is 6% of the 683 B left rather than 12%. Option 02 is the value pick if 16 B matters more than the enclosure reading — but note it is the one option that is hard to tell from Field at tile size. Option 04 loses at the size it is mostly seen.
