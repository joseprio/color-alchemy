THE WHEAT ICON

U+1F33E is SHEAF OF RICE, and it is drawn like one. Six ways to fix that, four of them s: SVGs in the flat Noto manner — solid fills, no outline, no gradient, one readable silhouette. Each is shown at the three sizes the game renders .s at, then dropped in beside the crops it has to stay distinct from.

WHAT IS ACTUALLY WRONG

Rice droops, wheat stands. A rice panicle bends away from the stem and the grains hang off it; a wheat ear is stiff and upright with the grains pressed flat against the stalk and awns spraying past the tip. At 32 px the bend is the only thing that survives, so U+1F33E reads rice to anyone who knows the difference and grass to everyone else.

THE FARMER KEEPS IT, AND SHOULD. The Farmer is \u{1F9D1}\u{200D}\u{1F33E} — there the grain is a prop in a person's hand and only has to read as "crop". This is only about the glyph standing alone as the crop itself.

THE SECOND SVG IN THE TABLE. Matter is the first and currently the only s: element. Everything an SVG option costs here is the artwork alone — the <svg class="s" viewBox="0 0 32 32"> wrapper is already in iconHtml() and is paid for.

VERDICT

03 SHIPPED — 04 when there is room

WHAT ACTUALLY SHIPPED: option 03, the plain ear, at 13295 B with 17 B of headroom. The Wheat is the second s: element in the table after Matter. The awns of option 04 are still the better drawing and are still 25 B out of reach; the entry's comment in src/elements.ts says so, so whoever frees those bytes knows what to spend them on.

Option 04 is the right artwork and it does not fit. The awns are not decoration — they are the single feature that separates wheat from rice at 32 px, and they cost three straight strokes. They also cost +146 B, which takes the page to 13337 and 25 B over the 13312 budget. Measured, not estimated.

03 FITS, AND BARELY. The same ear without the awns is +104 B — 13295, seventeen bytes of headroom left for everything that comes after it. It is affordable in the arithmetic sense and reckless in every other one, and what it buys is a generic grain: upright and gold, which is a real improvement on rice, but not specifically wheat.

THE ACTUAL RECOMMENDATION IS TO RE-FIT FIRST. fn-order.json and rr-config.json are both stale right now and the reorder pass is switched off entirely, so every number on this page was measured against a build that is leaving bytes on the floor. The reorder alone has been worth −49 B historically. Run npm run fn-order-optimize and npm run roadroller-optimize, then re-measure 04: it needs 25 B and the re-fit plausibly returns twice that. That is the order to do this in, and it costs no artwork at all.

05 AND 06 LOSE ON THEIR OWN MERITS, budget aside. Two ears and three ears are lovely at 110 px and mush at 32 px, which is the size that decides. Keep them in mind for a Sheaf or a Harvest element — the ear will already be drawn, and the <defs>/<use> construction makes each extra copy cost about forty characters.

AND IF NOTHING FITS, the honest fallback is not 02 — it is to leave the Wheat on U+1F33E knowingly and write the reason down, the way the table records every other trade it takes. "The glyph is named for the wrong grain and we could not afford 104 B to say so" is a perfectly good comment, and it is the one this experiment exists to let someone write.
